§ SERVICES — FIVE LINES, ONE STANDARD

What we build. And how we build it.

Five focused service lines. Each one delivered by a seasoned team with the same precision, transparency, and uncompromising craft.

01 — AI AGENTS & AUTOMATION

Automate the decisions your team keeps making manually.

What it is

Custom AI agents that operate autonomously inside your business — retrieving data, making decisions, triggering actions, and reporting back. Built on leading LLMs (Claude, GPT-4, Gemini) with tool-calling, memory, and multi-step reasoning. These are not chatbots. They are software systems that think.

Who needs this

Operations-heavy businesses drowning in manual processes. Healthcare platforms needing patient triage automation. Fintech companies automating KYC, collections or customer support. Logistics firms handling delivery exceptions at scale.

What's included

  • Agent architecture design tailored to your workflow
  • Full tool integrations (CRM, database, APIs, communication channels)
  • Human-in-the-loop controls and override mechanisms
  • Monitoring dashboard with alert thresholds
  • Complete technical documentation and handover session

Examples of agents we build

  • Patient intake and triage agent for a telemedicine platform
  • KYC document verification agent for an NBFC
  • Delivery exception-handling and escalation agent for logistics
  • Lead qualification and CRM-update agent for a B2B sales team
  • Service recovery analysis agent for a healthcare enterprise
02 — INTELLIGENT CHATBOTS

Conversations that actually resolve problems.

What it is

Context-aware chatbots deployed on WhatsApp, your website, or your mobile app. Not keyword-matching FAQ bots. LLM-powered conversations that understand intent, retrieve real data from your systems, and resolve queries end to end — without a human in the loop for routine requests.

Who needs this

Any business receiving repetitive customer queries: clinics, diagnostic labs, edtech platforms, ecommerce brands, banks, insurance companies. If your support team answers the same 20 questions every day, a Clarix.intel chatbot handles them — accurately and instantly.

What's included

  • Conversation design and intent mapping for your use case
  • LLM backend with RAG for accurate, context-grounded answers
  • WhatsApp Business API integration or web embed
  • Multilingual support (English, Hindi + regional languages available)
  • Analytics dashboard: resolution rate, volume, drop-off, escalation triggers
  • Human handoff protocol when complex queries require it

Examples

  • WhatsApp appointment booking and reminder bot for a diagnostic chain
  • 24/7 patient query resolution bot for a hospital network
  • Student doubt-solving and course guidance bot for an edtech platform
  • Post-purchase support and returns bot for a D2C brand
  • Internal HR policy chatbot for a mid-size enterprise
03 — WEBSITES & ONLINE PLATFORMS

Your business, fully online. Storefront, payments, bots, and operations in one platform.

What it is

An end-to-end digital presence built for businesses that need to operate, sell, and serve online. We build the whole stack: a brand-tailored website, payment gateway integration, domain and email setup, an admin panel for inventory and orders, a WhatsApp ordering or inquiry bot, an LLM-powered customer service bot, and an optional mobile app. One firm, one timeline, one bill — instead of stitching together five vendors.

Who needs this

Small businesses ready to move past Instagram DMs, WhatsApp screenshots, and Excel order sheets. Mid-market companies replacing fragmented tools with one cohesive platform. Service businesses needing booking + payments + reminders. Brands launching D2C. Retailers digitising operations. Any business whose customers expect to find, buy from, and message them online.

Industries we build for

  • Retail & D2C — clothing, accessories, beauty, food, electronics
  • Healthcare — clinics, diagnostic labs, pharmacies, wellness centres
  • Financial services — advisory, lending platforms, insurance brokers
  • Hospitality & services — salons, restaurants, agencies, consultancies
  • Education — coaching centres, courses, K-12, upskilling platforms
  • B2B & manufacturing — showrooms, distributors, dealer networks

What's included

  • Custom-designed website tailored to your brand and industry
  • Payment gateway integration (Razorpay, Stripe, PayU, COD)
  • Domain registration, DNS setup, professional email
  • WhatsApp ordering or inquiry bot with order-status updates
  • LLM-powered customer service bot for product, service, and FAQ queries
  • Admin panel — inventory, orders, customers, payouts, reports
  • Email + SMS notifications for orders and customer events
  • Optional Android / cross-platform mobile app for staff or customers
  • SEO foundations, analytics, uptime monitoring, security hardening
  • Full documentation, knowledge transfer, and post-launch support

Example platforms we've built

  • D2C fashion brand — storefront, Razorpay checkout, admin, Android app
  • Clinic chain — appointment booking, payments, WhatsApp reminders
  • Insurance broker — lead-capture site, policy library, customer-service bot
  • Coaching institute — course catalogue, enrolment, parent-portal bot
  • Boutique retailer — product catalogue, COD + UPI, WhatsApp order desk

How we tier the build

  • Starter: single-category storefront, basic payments, one WhatsApp bot, simple admin. Built for solo founders and single-location businesses.
  • Standard: full commerce features, mobile app option, two bots (orders + customer service), inventory bulk-import, multi-user admin. Built for growing brands and mid-market operators.
  • Enterprise: multi-tenant or multi-location, complex workflows, deeper integrations (ERP, accounting, logistics), custom analytics. Built for established businesses at scale.

Turn your data from a liability into an asset.

What it is

End-to-end data engineering — from ingestion and transformation to model serving and monitoring. ETL pipelines, vector databases for AI retrieval systems, real-time streaming architectures, and ML model deployment wrapped in clean, production-grade APIs.

Who needs this

Companies sitting on data they can't use. AI startups needing a reliable, scalable backend. Enterprises replacing manual Excel reporting with real-time intelligence dashboards. Any team that's built a model in a notebook but can't get it into production.

What's included

Examples

  • RAG knowledge base powering an insurance company's internal chatbot
