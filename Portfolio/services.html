02 — INTELLIGENT CHATBOTS

Conversations that actually resolve problems.

Timeline2–4 weeks
Investment₹75K – ₹2L
Global$1,000 – $2,500

What it is

Context-aware chatbots deployed on WhatsApp, your website, or your mobile app. Not keyword-matching FAQ bots. LLM-powered conversations that understand intent, retrieve real data from your systems, and resolve queries end to end — without a human in the loop for routine requests.

Who needs this

Any business receiving repetitive customer queries: clinics, diagnostic labs, edtech platforms, ecommerce brands, banks, insurance companies. If your support team answers the same 20 questions every day, a Clarix.intel chatbot handles them — accurately and instantly.

What's included

  • Conversation design and intent mapping for your use case
  • LLM backend with RAG for accurate, context-grounded answers
  • WhatsApp Business API integration or web embed
  • Multilingual support (English, Hindi + regional languages available)
  • Analytics dashboard: resolution rate, volume, drop-off, escalation triggers
  • Human handoff protocol when complex queries require it

Examples

  • WhatsApp appointment booking and reminder bot for a diagnostic chain
  • 24/7 patient query resolution bot for a hospital network
  • Student doubt-solving and course guidance bot for an edtech platform
  • Post-purchase support and returns bot for a D2C brand
  • Internal HR policy chatbot for a mid-size enterprise

Turn your data from a liability into an asset.

Timeline3–8 weeks
ETL / data pipeline₹1.5L – ₹6L
RAG / GenAI build₹6L – ₹15L
Global$2,000 – $18,000

What it is

End-to-end data engineering — from ingestion and transformation to model serving and monitoring. ETL pipelines, vector databases for AI retrieval systems, real-time streaming architectures, and ML model deployment wrapped in clean, production-grade APIs.

Who needs this

Companies sitting on data they can't use. AI startups needing a reliable, scalable backend. Enterprises replacing manual Excel reporting with real-time intelligence dashboards. Any team that's built a model in a notebook but can't get it into production.

What's included

Examples

  • RAG knowledge base powering an insurance company's internal chatbot
  • Data ingestion pipeline unifying 5 source systems into a single dashboard
  • ML model backend for a credit-scoring feature in a fintech lending app
  • Automated reporting pipeline replacing weekly manual Excel exports
