§ ABOUT — OUR STORY

Built by an engineer who got tired of agencies that overpromise.

Clarix.intel was founded in 2026 by a senior AI engineer based in Bengaluru — built to be everything agencies aren't.

THE WHY

Clarix.intel was founded in 2026 in Bengaluru. After years of working inside large organisations and watching good projects fail — not because of bad ideas, but because of unclear scopes, junior handoffs, and teams that optimised for billable hours over outcomes — Clarix.intel was built to be different.

The name says it simply: Clarity. In how we scope. In how we communicate. In what we deliver.

We are not a 50-person agency with a sales team and an offshore delivery floor. We are a focused team of senior engineers — deliberately small, deeply experienced, and built so delivery never slips. You speak directly with the people building your product — before, during, and after delivery. There is no account manager between you and the engineers writing your code, and no compromise on quality as projects scale.

Every client engagement follows three non-negotiable principles:

Honesty before the sale. If a simpler solution solves your problem, we'll tell you — even if it means a smaller project. Trust is worth more than a single invoice.

Transparency during delivery. You receive a written update every Friday. You always know what was built, what's next, and what risks exist. No surprises.

Quality that lasts. We write documented, tested, maintainable code. When the project ends, you own software your own team can understand — not a black box that only we can touch.
